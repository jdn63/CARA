Based on the strategic risk assessment for {{ risk_data.location }}, this action plan provides structured guidance for your county emergency management agency's annual all-hazards preparedness and mitigation planning. Actions focus on emergency management roles including hazard mitigation, response coordination, continuity of operations, public warning, and community resilience. The plan aligns with FEMA Hazard Mitigation Plan elements, FEMA Core Capabilities, and CISA Cybersecurity Performance Goals as appropriate, and is intended to inform updates to your jurisdiction's hazard mitigation plan and emergency operations plan.

Planning Mode: Actions prioritized for annual strategic planning using the Emergency Management weighting profile, which emphasizes natural-hazard exposure and infrastructure impact. All activities reflect county emergency management agency roles and responsibilities under the National Response Framework and Wisconsin Emergency Management coordination.
