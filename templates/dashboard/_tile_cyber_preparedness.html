Cyber Preparedness Planning Resource — Not Scored

Why there is no score: no authoritative county-level cybersecurity incident data exists for Wisconsin. CARA previously displayed a proxy-modeled score here; it was retired in August 2026 because it could not be tied to real local measurements. Cyber preparedness still matters — this panel provides actionable steps and authoritative resources instead of a number.

{% call col.section('cyber-prep-checklist', 'Preparedness Checklist for Health Departments') %}
  • Downtime procedures: documented manual workflows for WEDSS, WIR, and EHR outages (paper forms, offline lookups).
  • Offline contact roster: printed or off-network copies of staff, partner, and vendor emergency contacts.
  • Backups: critical data backed up, kept offline or immutable, and restoration tested at least annually.
  • Incident response plan: who isolates systems, who notifies whom (IT, leadership, insurers, reporting agencies), and in what order.
  • Exercise: run a cyber tabletop at least annually — CISA publishes free tabletop exercise packages.
  • MS-ISAC membership: free for local governments; provides alerts, advisories, and incident support.
{% endcall %}
Authoritative Resources
  • CISA — alerts, healthcare-sector advisories, tabletop exercise packages, and free cyber hygiene services.
  • HHS HC3 — Health Sector Cybersecurity Coordination Center threat briefs.
  • MS-ISAC — no-cost cyber threat intelligence and incident support for state and local government.
  • HHS OCR Breach Portal — reported healthcare breaches affecting 500+ individuals (filterable to Wisconsin).
This panel is guidance, not an assessment. Jurisdictions seeking a cyber risk evaluation should engage CISA’s regional cybersecurity advisor or conduct a dedicated assessment.
